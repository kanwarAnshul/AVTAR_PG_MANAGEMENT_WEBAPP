# AVTAR Singh PG — Privacy Policy (v1.0)

> **Owner note:** This is the built-in template shown to tenants and wardens until you publish your own text in **Owner → Settings → Privacy Policy**. Because it is a template, have it reviewed for your actual data practices before public launch. Publishing your own text in the app replaces what users see; changing it does not reset Terms acceptance.

We collect only the data needed to run your stay: name, photo, contact details, KYC documents, rent and complaint records.

- KYC documents are stored in encrypted storage with signed access links (valid for 60 seconds).
- PAN numbers are encrypted at rest with AES-256; only the owner can view them.
- Location and presence data are used solely for safety and operations.
- We never sell personal data. You may request export or deletion at any time via the owner office.

## How we use the data

| Data | Purpose | Who can see it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Name, photo, phone, address | Tenant identity and emergency contact | Owner, assigned warden, the tenant |
| KYC documents (ID, address proof, PAN) | Verification before move-in | Owner; warden during review only |
| Rent, deposits, invoices | Billing and cash-flow reporting | Owner, warden, the tenant |
| Complaints and requests | Resolve issues on the property | Owner, warden, the tenant |
| Daily presence / emergency alerts | Safety and operations | Owner, warden |
| Terms acceptance timestamp | Legal record of consent | Owner (audit), the tenant |

## Retention and deletion

- Active tenant records stay while the tenancy is active.
- On approved checkout the tenant is **archived first** (contact and feedback retained by the owner), then personal documents, invoices, registration, tenancy and the login are permanently deleted.
- Rejected registrations are retained with a mandatory rejection reason for audit.
- Former-tenant archive entries are owner-only.

## Your rights

You may ask the office for a copy of your data, correction of inaccurate details, or deletion where retention is no longer required. Requests are handled by the owner office at `avtar.singh@avtarpg.com`.

## Children and sensitive data

The service is intended for adults. Do not upload documents of minors except where required by law and with guardian consent. Aadhaar/PAN are sensitive identifiers; upload only what is required for verification.
